# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Lebanese family shop owner operating from a phone at the counter. Non-technical user, primary language Arabic. Uses the app throughout the working day in a physical shop environment — glancing at phone between serving customers, working fast. Secondary users: none (single account).

## Product Purpose

Invoice tracking and client debt management for a small retail shop. The owner creates invoices for credit customers, records payments, and sees at a glance who owes what and to whom. Success: create an invoice in under 15 seconds, record a payment in under 20 seconds, never ask "how much does this client owe?"

## Brand Personality

Clear · Trusted · Fast. Sharp tool that respects the owner's time. Not a toy, not a banking portal.

## Anti-references

- **Cheap POS systems**: cluttered, tiny text, Windows-era grids, information overload
- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: identical metric cards, startup aesthetic, endless whitespace
- **WhatsApp / green theme**: not a messaging app

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — every element earns its place. No ornament.
2. **Arabic-first** — layout, type, and rhythm designed for RTL. Latin fallback, not bolted on.
3. **Thumb-native** — primary actions always reachable in the bottom 40% of a phone screen.
4. **Numbers are sacred** — currency amounts unmistakably readable. Tabular figures, consistent alignment, USD vs L.L. never ambiguous.
5. **Fast paths** — creating an invoice for a known client: 3 taps before typing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Arabic rendered with a proper Arabic font (Cairo), not system fallback
- Minimum touch target 44×44px for all interactive elements
- WCAG AA baseline; WCAG AAA for currency amounts and client names
- Voice input for descriptions must be visually obvious
- Color is never the sole status indicator (unpaid/partial/paid use icon + label too)
